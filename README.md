## Customer Support Platform

A technology demonstrator for a production-style, full-stack business application.

This project explores the design and implementation of a realistic customer support and case-management platform. The application provides a practical business domain in which to demonstrate modern application architecture, development practices, testing, deployment, and operational concerns.

The platform will support a complete customer-service case lifecycle, including:

- Customer case submission and communication
- Email-to-case ingestion
- Case routing, queues, and assignment
- Agent case management and collaboration
- Internal notes and customer communication
- Macros and high-volume queue operations
- SLA tracking and risk visibility
- Role-based access and administration
- Audit history
- Real-time case and queue updates
- Search and filtering
